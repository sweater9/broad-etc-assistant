How to Use the ETF Assistant

A simple monthly workflow for broad-based UCITS ETF decision support.

← Back to dashboard
Start here: the tool is designed to help allocate a regular contribution among eligible broad-market ETFs. It is educational decision support, not regulated investment advice, and it does not place trades.

Recommended monthly workflow

1. Enter your monthly contribution

At the top of the dashboard, enter the amount available to invest this month. The default is AED 700, but you can change it at any time.

2. Update your portfolio

Under Portfolio, enter the current AED market value of CSPX, EIMI and WSML and press Save portfolio. Alternatively, use the IBKR JSON import if you have a compatible positions response.

3. Refresh market signals

Go to Market → Refresh price signals. Check the Data Source Health and Data Quality panels. If the feed is stale, degraded or unavailable, treat the recommendation cautiously and use the manual price-history fallback if needed.

4. Get this month's decision

Press Get this month's decision. The dashboard answers three questions: whether to invest, which eligible broad ETF is preferred, and how the contribution should be allocated. The score combines valuation, trend, portfolio underweight, risk and market context; confidence is shown separately.

5. Review contribution impact

Check Contribution Impact before acting. It shows how the proposed purchase changes the portfolio allocation relative to the current 65% CSPX / 20% EIMI / 15% WSML core target.

6. Check opportunity and confidence

The Opportunity Monitor uses drawdown and trend together. A correction can make an ETF more attractive, but a falling price alone is not treated as a buy signal. Low data confidence should reduce how much weight you give the recommendation.

7. Save the monthly review

After reviewing the decision, press Save this month's review. This records the recommendation, contribution, holdings, confidence and available market observations in your browser so you can compare decisions over time.

How to read the main sections

  • This Month's Decision: the main proposed action and decision score.
  • Eligible Universe: ranks broad UCITS ETFs; alternatives are research candidates and do not automatically replace the core portfolio.
  • Allocation Drift: identifies which core holding is furthest below its target weight.
  • Market Snapshot: shows stored price returns, 52-week high and drawdown information.
  • Recommendation Audit: records what inputs drove previous decisions.
  • Backtest / Stress / Projection: research tools for understanding historical behaviour and hypothetical outcomes; they are not forecasts.

Important limitations

Market feeds can fail or become stale. Valuation snapshots may update less frequently than prices. Backtests do not guarantee future results. Price returns may exclude distributions, FX, tax and transaction costs. The tool deliberately restricts itself to broad-market ETF decision support and does not automatically trade through IBKR.
